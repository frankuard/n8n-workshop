Part 1: Basic Automation

Build your first n8n automation by connecting a student form to Gmail.

1. Part One: Basic Automation

In this part, we will create our first simple automation using n8n.

The goal is to allow a student to submit a form and automatically send that information through Gmail.

Basic idea:

Student fills the form → n8n receives the information → Gmail sends the email automatically.

2. Create the Student Form

The first step is to create a form that collects the information required from the student.

Form Fields

Field Type Example
Email Email faculty@gmail.com
Department / Faculty Dropdown SSD
Email Subject Text Leave Request
Message Textarea I am feeling sick and need leave for today's class.

Department Options

  • SSD
  • RTE
  • Student Service
  • Finance Department
  • IT Department

3. Connect the Form to Gmail

Once the form has been created, we can connect it to Gmail.

Student Form → n8n → Gmail

Gmail Configuration

Add a Gmail node after the form node.

Connected Account

Example:

example123@gmail.com

Recipient

For the workshop demonstration, you can use:

roshankarki4956@gmail.com

Email Subject

Map the form's Email Subject field to the Gmail subject field. 

{{ Email Subject }}

Email Body

Map the student's Message field to the Gmail body. 

{{ Message }}

Basic Automation Flow

Student fills form → n8n receives information → Gmail → Email sent

This is our first automation.

The student does not need to manually open Gmail and compose an email.

n8n handles that process automatically.

Test the Workflow

  1. Open the form.
  2. Enter a student's email.
  3. Select a department.
  4. Enter an email subject.
  5. Enter a message.
  6. Submit the form.
  7. Check the Gmail inbox.
Success!

If the email arrives automatically, your first n8n automation is working.
